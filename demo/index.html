Customer app · Android & iOS

Send a package in under 30 seconds.

  1. Tap Send a package, pick two areas, see the price instantly.
  2. Pay with card (test mode), or choose transfer or cash.
  3. Watch a verified rider move on the live map.
  4. Or tap Ask TTC AI and type “abeg carry food from Garki go Lugbe”.

Tip: open Side by side to accept your own order as the rider.

TTC Business

Rider app · works on low-cost Android

Clear jobs, clear earnings.

  1. Jobs booked in the Customer app or Business portal appear here.
  2. Accept, then tap Navigate to open Google Maps.
  3. Confirm pickup, then enter the receiver's 4-digit code to finish.
  4. Check Earnings and Profile for payouts and verification.

No jobs showing? Book one in the Customer tab first. It waits here for you.
