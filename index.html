01 · What you're building

Not just a delivery app. A delivery network for businesses.

Restaurants, pharmacies, small businesses and vendors enter what they need delivered, and TTC connects a verified rider to deliver it. Individuals can send packages too. Three groups have to love the product at the same time:

🏪

Senders

Businesses sending every day, and customers sending now and then. They want a fair price up front, a rider fast, and to see exactly where their package is.

🏍️

Riders

Verified riders who want a steady stream of jobs, clear directions, and to see what they've earned. A simple app that works on lower-cost Android phones.

🧭

TTC operations

One screen to see every delivery, approve riders, set prices, confirm payments and spot problems before customers complain.

Why the Business Portal is in the first build, not a later phase.

Businesses are where repeat orders come from. A pharmacy that sends 15 deliveries a day is worth more than hundreds of one-time customers. So from launch day, businesses get their own portal: saved pickup addresses, several deliveries at once, history and monthly statements.

What wins in this market

  1. Speed. A rider assigned in minutes and an honest arrival time.
  2. Trust. Verified riders, live tracking, and a delivery code so packages reach the right person.
  3. Easy payment. Card, transfer or cash. No chasing payments over WhatsApp.
  4. Repeat business. Merchants who find TTC easier than calling their usual rider.

12 · Questions

Answers before you ask.

Something not covered? Ask the proposal assistant (bottom right). It answers from this proposal only, and anything it can't answer comes straight to Hussein.

What exactly do we get in the free 2 weeks?

Real work on your platform: backend, accounts and login, the customer "send a package" flow with automatic pricing, and the admin pricing screen. It runs as a test app on your own Android phones and iPhones, so you can judge the quality and how we communicate before you pay anything.

What if we decide not to continue after 2 weeks?

You keep all the code we've written (it's already in a repository in TTC's name) and you owe nothing. No pressure and no hard feelings.

What happens if you're late?

If a milestone is late because of us, that milestone's payment is refunded. The clock pauses only for delays outside our control: waiting for your feedback or content, Apple, Google or Paystack account verification, or store review times.

How can you finish in 6 weeks?

A team of four working in parallel, one React Native codebase for both Android and iOS, and proven building blocks for login, payments, maps and notifications. AI-assisted development speeds up routine work, and every feature is still reviewed and tested by a person. And we've already started: the demo exists.

Who owns the code, the apps and the accounts?

TTC does, from day one. The code repository, Play Store and App Store listings, cloud hosting and Paystack account are all in TTC's name.

Why choose Build + Year over Build?

For ₦1.5M more (about ₦125,000 a month) you get 12 months of support and updates, plus the features that help TTC grow after launch: WhatsApp ordering, a ChatGPT app, analytics, scheduled deliveries, automated rider payouts, refunds, and Shopify/WooCommerce plug-ins for online vendors. If we ever stop supporting you, unused months are refunded.

Our designer is working on designs. Will you use them?

Yes. We build your designer's approved designs as supplied. The demo is a prototype to show the experience. If some screens aren't ready, our senior frontend engineer designs them in your style so the build isn't held up.

What isn't included in the price?

Apple ($99/year) and Google ($25 once) developer accounts, and running costs paid straight to providers: hosting, domain, Google Maps, SMS codes, AI usage beyond free tiers and Paystack transaction fees. We give you a monthly estimate in week 1.

What if we want new features during the build?

We note them and add them to the roadmap (included on the Build + Year plan where they're on the list, otherwise quoted before any work starts). This protects your launch date.

Do riders need expensive phones?

No. The rider app is tested on lower-cost Android phones and is designed to work with patchy mobile data.

How does the AI cost work?

The AI runs on Google Gemini, which has a free tier that covers early usage. As TTC grows, usage costs are small per conversation and are included in the monthly estimate we give you in week 1. The app works fully without AI, so it's never a single point of failure.

What if Apple rejects the app?

Fixing issues raised in the first store reviews is part of the build. We prepare privacy, data-safety and location-permission information carefully up front to avoid the common rejections.

What happens after launch on the Build plan?

60 days of bug-fix support is included. After that you can move to the Year plan or call us for work as needed. Either way the code is yours and documented, so any developer can continue.

What if Hussein is unavailable?

Forge Growth is a team of four, so work continues. The code is documented and lives in TTC's repository, so you are never dependent on one person.
